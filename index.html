At home on your Mac.

Installers, release notes & SHA-256 checksums ↗

  1. Open the latest release. Choose macos-arm64 for Apple Silicon (M-series), or macos-x86_64 for Intel.
  2. Open the DMG and drag Glim into Applications.
  3. Releases are ad-hoc signed, without Apple notarization. If macOS blocks launch, review the download source and use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.

Signed in-app updates are available. Office layout and media support depend on macOS; some formats provide limited, read-only previews.
